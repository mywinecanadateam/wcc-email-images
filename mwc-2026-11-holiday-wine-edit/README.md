# MWC — The Holiday Wine Edit (2026-11)

My Wine Canada campaign assets. Bottle photos come from the MWC Shopify product images (cdn.shopify.com), cut out with `studio-cutout.py` and passed through `review-cutouts.py`.

- `bottle-shots-raw/`, `bottle-shots/`, `bottle-shots/email/` (420 px tall, for email cards)
- `pack-groups/holiday-wine-edit-6.png`: transparent 6-bottle group (`pack_group.py`)
- `backgrounds/higgsfield-holiday-table.png`: AI-generated scene, **no bottles in it**; the real bottles are composited on top
- `hero-composites/holiday-wine-edit-hero*.jpg`: the email hero

Flag: `2023-megalomaniac-to-be-frank-cabernet-franc.png` comes from the 2022 listing's bottle photo. The front label is the same and shows no vintage, because MWC and the winery only publish label art for the 2023 vintage.
