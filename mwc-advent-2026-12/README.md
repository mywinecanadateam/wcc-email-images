# MWC 12 Days of Canadian Wine, December 2026

Images for My Wine Canada's advent emails (Dec 1-12, 2026), hosted here so Klaviyo can use them by raw URL.
Lineup source: `working-docs/mwc-advent-calendar-2026.md` in the brain repo ("The bundles" table).

- `heroes/` the 12 Higgsfield hero scenes (label-checked), `day-NN-hero-1200.jpg`.
- `packs/` one transparent PNG per 3-pack and 6-pack, real bottle photos, bottom-aligned, in the doc's order. Half bottles shrunk to size (375 mL 0.78, 200 mL 0.65, 500 mL O'Port 0.82).
- `packs/_needs-decision/` Day 5 Bench 1775 packs: built with 2018-label photos of Red Meritage and Cabernet Franc (the listings sell 2020). Not for use until someone decides.
- `bottle-shots/` transparent cutouts, `<vintage>-<wine>.png`; `bottle-shots-raw/` the photos as served, with `images-manifest.json` (source URL, label check, cutout method, mismatches, reuse).
